Phishing.Database (phishing links and domains)
https://github.com/Phishing-Database/Phishing.Database
Files downloaded by this iPhone from https://phish.co.za/latest/ when "Known-phishing lists" is on
(Phishing.Database is on by default once the lists are on). Matched on this phone only; nothing about
your links is sent.

Provenance: Phishing.Database aggregates several upstream sources that the project does not all name,
so the origin of every entry has not been confirmed. A match is shown as evidence with its source
("Phishing.Database"), never as the verdict of a named authority.
